Meet the Owners

Karl Albrecht & Jeff Chandler

Construction Roots & Insight

Not Just Installers — Veterans

Karl and Jeff aren't just insulation installers; they are construction veterans. Their deep backgrounds in other trades give them an unparalleled advantage: they can accurately assess an upcoming job site upon arrival to determine exactly if a site is truly ready for insulation work.

That trade fluency means fewer surprises, fewer callbacks, and a crew that speaks the same language as your General Contractor from day one.

Leadership

Founded on Experience

Co-Founder
Karl Albrecht

Karl brings four decades of construction experience across nearly every trade — starting in drywall, then moving through modulars, vinyl siding, and roofing, with time as an accomplished plumber and steamfitter along the way. Today he installs spray foam himself, giving him firsthand knowledge of the right conditions for application and for the crew doing the work.

Co-Founder
Jeff Chandler

With almost three decades in construction, Jeff built an extensive drywall background, running his own drywall company for over 20 years before founding Premium Insulation — a natural next step, since insulation and drywall go back-to-back in the build phase. Experience in roofing and framing rounds out his background, and he's installed spray foam himself to know what a job well done requires.
